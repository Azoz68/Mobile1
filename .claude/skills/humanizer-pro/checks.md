# Humanizer Pro self-check

Run every check after the edit. Answer each pass or fail. Fix any failure and
run the list again before returning the draft.

For a detect request, only the Detect mode section applies.

## Fidelity (outranks everything below)

If any check in this section fails, revert that passage to its original wording
and flag it to the author, even at the cost of a rougher draft.

1. Does the claim inventory match in both directions? Every claim in the source
   appears in the output, and the output contains no claim absent from the
   source. Added counter-arguments, implications, and examples are the most
   common breach.
2. Has any verb of assertion shifted strength (`shows`, `suggests`, `proves`,
   `may indicate`), and has any hedge been added to or removed from a sentence?
3. Is every number, unit, percentage, date, sample size, statistic, citation,
   and proper noun character-for-character identical?
4. Does every technical term appear in its original form, with the same term
   used for the same concept throughout?
5. Was any explicit statement demoted to an implication, merged away, or
   dropped?
6. Were gaps in the author's reasoning reported rather than filled in?

## Register

1. Was the register identified before editing, and were the register-gated
   rules applied accordingly?
2. In posture A, was hedging left intact, passive voice left where the venue
   expects it, and no opinion, first person, humor, or deliberate roughness
   added?
3. In posture A, were words on the watch list kept when they function as terms
   of art in the field?
4. In posture C, was existing voice preserved and foregrounded rather than
   replaced with neutral prose, and was no character invented that the author
   never showed?

## Editing principles

1. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual problem, with no
   aggressive compression that strips character?
2. Are strong sentences left alone rather than rewritten for consistency, and
   is the draft free of the tidiness that makes every paragraph the same?
3. Does the draft preserve the author's distinctive vocabulary, cadence,
   bluntness, humor, uncertainty, and digressions?
4. Does it lead with what the reader needs while keeping personal setup that
   adds context, tension, or character?
5. Do sentences earn their place, with concrete facts protected and direct
   verbs used where the draft supports them?
6. Does every generic sentence pass the portability test, or was it cut or made
   specific from material already in the source?
7. Is the substance and nuance intact, with only the hard-to-read structure
   stripped?
8. Are genuinely tangled sentences fixed while clear spoken cadence, fragments,
   and shifts in pace remain?
9. Was the structure preserved unless it was hurting the piece, and was any
   reorganization explained?

## Patterns

1. Inflation removed: significance puffery, promotional language, superficial
   `-ing` clauses, notability emphasis, aphorism formulas?
2. Fake-profound kickers deleted rather than rewritten into better metaphors?
3. Interpretive metadiscourse removed, including reader guidance, emphasis
   markers, and redundant glossing?
4. Weasel attribution replaced with a named source, or flagged when the author
   has none? No source invented?
5. Speculative gap-filling and cutoff disclaimers removed?
6. Binary contrasts, negative parallelism, negative listing, and tailing
   negations stated as direct positive claims?
7. Rule-of-three triplets, synonym cycling, false ranges, and copula avoidance
   fixed?
8. Throat-clearing, signposting, faux-insight setups, rhetorical setups, and
   conversational openers cut?
9. Colon reveals rewritten as plain sentences, with sentence case after colons
   unless grammar, a proper noun, a title, or code requires otherwise?
10. Dramatic fragmentation and manufactured punchlines resolved into complete
    sentences?
11. Recap endings and generic optimism cut, so the piece ends on a concrete
    point, takeaway, or next action?
12. Formulaic challenges sections, fragmented headers, and diff-anchored
    narration fixed?
13. Filler phrases, empty adverbs, redundant hedge stacking, and uniform
    hyphenation cleaned up?
14. Formatting slop removed: decorative bold, emoji headings, inline-header
    bullets that should be prose, title-case headings, headers over two-sentence
    sections?
15. Chatbot artifacts and sycophantic tone removed?

## Em dashes

1. Was the finished draft scanned for `—` and `–`, including spaced dashes and
   double hyphens?
2. Is the count zero in short copy, and at most one or two in long-form where
   each clearly beats every alternative?

## Final read

1. Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked
   punchy fragments?
2. Does sentence length actually vary, rather than settling into a uniform
   mid-length cadence?
3. Would the author recognize this as their own voice?
4. Would it sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?
5. Was the question *what still reads as machine-generated here?* asked and
   answered honestly before finalizing?
6. Does the output include the full edited draft, a **What changed** section,
   and an explicit note on any stylistic fix abandoned to protect meaning?
7. Is the output free of any claim about whether the result will pass or fail a
   detector?

## Detect mode

1. Is each finding named as a pattern from the catalogue, with the offending
   line quoted and a fix in a few words?
2. Is the draft left unrewritten?
3. Is there no score, no percentage, and no verdict on whether AI produced the
   text?
4. Were false-positive items left unflagged: isolated transitions, formal
   vocabulary that is not on the watch list, curly quotes alone, a single short
   sentence, and second-language phrasing?
5. Was an edit offered as a follow-up?
